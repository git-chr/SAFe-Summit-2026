### The Rugby Match in the Regulatory Rain: Scaling Adaptive Agility at Roche

- **Date:** March 24, 2026

- **Time:** 1:50 PM-2:25 PMCET
- **Type:** Customer Story
- **Location:** 1.1 : Administratiezaal
In regulated product development, safety often turns into slowness. Handoffs multiply. Validation becomes a queue. Teams learn late, when change is most expensive.

At our platform in Roche Diagnostics, we hit that wall in full force. Hardware, software, and compliance moved at different speeds, and every change felt like something to contain. In systems like this, fear doesn’t look dramatic. It shows up quietly: in silence, in late escalations, and in documentation written to protect rather than to learn.

We had to unlearn that inside the regulatory rain, speed is not an engineering problem first. It is a leadership problem.

We were never framework-dogmatic; we were pragmatic. In this session, we share how we shifted from a linear relay race to a fast, collaborative rugby match, delivering €4–6 million in annual savings and reducing time-to-market by 6–12 months.

We unpack the two sides of this shift:

• The Architecture of Safety: How specific choices (modular Shell/Core) reduced the blast radius of change, allowing us to innovate while keeping regulatory integrity.

• Making the Implicit Explicit: How we redefined Definition of Done as validated knowledge to bring risks and half-answers into the room early, preventing evidence work from disappearing into the shadows.

• AI as a Pragmatic Partner: How we used AI under tight headcount to draft evidence and trade-offs, forcing us to become radically explicit about what only humans are allowed to decide in regulated systems.

Join us for a story about the moments we all recognize too late, and how to build a system where those moments become speakable while there is still time.

- **Date:** March 25, 2026

- **Time:** 2:50 PM-3:25 PMCET
- **Type:** Breakout Session (SAFe & AI)
- **Location:** 1.20 : Veilingzaal
Everybody talks about AI and faster and better software delivery, but the real question is how. AI agents increase flow not by replacing people, but can foster collaboration with keeping humans in the loop. For example, when AI supports the analysis and preparation of features, refinement becomes faster, clearer, and more focused.

In this practical experience report, Nils Hyoma shows how custom GitHub Copilot Agents are used in the IDE and in the cloud to analyze backlog items and existing code, uncover assumptions, and identify blind spots before refinement.

The interactive, demo-driven session features a custom GPT and a custom AI Agent combining Problem Personas and BDD to challenge requirements from different failure perspectives and turn risks into concrete scenarios. Participants experience how AI improves collaboration, reduces meeting time, and increases flow, without losing human ownership.

#### Key Takeaways:

In this session I will learn how AI agents improve flow by strengthening collaboration in refinement—while keeping humans in the loop
In this session I will learn how custom GitHub Copilot Agents analyze backlog items and code to uncover blind spots before refinement meetings
In this session I will learn how Problem Personas and AI-generated BDD scenarios turn risks into concrete, testable requirements

### Transforming Enterprise Data into Intelligence and Insights with RAG

- **Date:** March 25, 2026

- **Time:** 2:50 PM-3:25 PMCET
- **Type:** Breakout Session (AI)
- **Location:** 1.1 : Administratiezaal
Enterprises are realizing that Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) isn’t just a feature — it’s the backbone of production-ready AI systems. But implementing RAG at scale is far more complex than adding a vector database and calling it done. The real work lies in integrating retrieval with enterprise data infrastructure, governance models, and evolving user expectations. You’ll learn how to architect RAG as a living system — one that evolves through continuous feedback, evaluation pipelines, and hybrid retrieval (semantic + symbolic).   This session explores what successful Enterprise RAG looks like in practice — from architecture to adoption. We’ll break down the most common pitfalls (like context fragmentation, stale embeddings, and retrieval bias), and share the hard-earned best practices that separate proof-of-concept demos from truly dependable, enterprise-grade RAG systems.

#### Key Takeaways:

Understand why RAG is the foundation of enterprise AI systems, not just a bolt-on capability
Discover how to operationalize RAG with monitoring, feedback loops, and continuous evaluation
Gain a step-by-step roadmap for maturing from prototype to production across multiple business domains
