# SAFe Summit 2026 Attendee Categorization

## Classification rule (agreed)

- **Software** = companies primarily selling agile/PM/software tooling platforms.
- **Producing** = companies primarily delivering non-tool end products/services (physical or digital).
- **Digital Product Company** is a **segment inside Producing** (not inside Software).
